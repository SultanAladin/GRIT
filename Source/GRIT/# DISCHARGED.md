## Name Rationale

**DISCHARGED** captures explosive energy release and electrical intensity without being childish or war-themed. Technical, aggressive, single-word punch that fits open-world combat racing. Works like GRID or RIVALS - simple, powerful, memorable. The name implies both battery discharge (power/speed) and weapon discharge (combat), perfectly matching fast-paced racing with explosive mechanics.
